Key Data Findings

  • Mathematics Underperformance: Math is the lowest-performing subject nationally, with only 33.24% of candidates meeting/exceeding expectations and 12.66% failing to meet minimum benchmarks.
  • Female Literacy Superiority: Female learners demonstrate significant leads in language mastery (+13.47% gap in Kiswahili and +10.22% gap in English).
  • Regional Age Disparities: Coastal (Kilifi 64.9%, Kwale 64.8%) and ASAL counties (Garissa 63.4%) record overage learner rates 3x higher than Central counties (Kiambu 20.7%).

Strategic Recommendations

  • Targeted STEM Remediation: Implement foundational numeracy interventions in Grades 7–9 to align math performance with strong scientific literacy (Integrated Sci at 80.17%).
  • Early Entry & ASAL Support: Address late school entry, repetition, and socio-economic drivers causing 60%+ overage progression in Coastal/Arid zones.
  • Boy-Child Literacy Engagement: Introduce tailored reading interventions to bridge the significant language attainment gap among male learners.
